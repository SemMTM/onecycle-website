About OneCycle

Science and sustainability, working as one.

Advancing sustainable technology

A science-driven clean-technology company transforming hazardous industrial materials into valuable, reusable resources.

What guides us

Founded on the belief that science and sustainability should work hand in hand, we combine precision engineering, environmental responsibility and innovation to create real, measurable impact.

Our core principles

01

Science-driven

Evidence, testing and technical rigour sit behind every decision we make.

02

Sustainable

Environmental performance is designed into the process, not added afterwards.

03

Integrity

We work with transparency, accountability and a commitment to verifiable results.

04

Circular economy

We see industrial residues as resources with the potential to re-enter productive use.

Our mission

Turning environmental challenges into sustainable opportunities.

We exist to protect the environment through science and precision.

Our mission is to develop recovery and refining technologies that eliminate hazardous waste, reduce emissions and unlock economic value from materials that would otherwise be lost.

Every project is approached with integrity, accountability and technical excellence, helping industry move toward a more circular and responsible model.

Our philosophy

Where science meets responsibility.

OneCycle was founded to align technological innovation with environmental stewardship. Our work is grounded in evidence, precision and long-term responsibility, because sustainability should be measurable rather than assumed.

We collaborate with selected partners who share our commitment to integrity, transparency and purpose-driven innovation. The goal is not visibility for its own sake; it is verified, repeatable progress.

Experience

25+ years of combined expertise in sustainable materials recovery.

Our team brings together materials science, chemistry and environmental engineering, with decades of combined research and industrial application.

That experience has shaped proprietary systems capable of delivering more than 90% reduction in hazardous waste and near-zero in-house waste generation. Continued collaboration helps us push sustainable refining and remediation further.
