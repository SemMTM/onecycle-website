About OneCycle

Advancing sustainable technologies for a cleaner future.

OneCycle is a science-driven clean-technology company transforming hazardous industrial materials into valuable, reusable resources. Founded on the belief that science and sustainability should work hand in hand, we combine precision engineering, environmental responsibility, and innovation to create real, measurable impact.

Our Core Principles

  • Science-Driven
  • Sustainable
  • Integrity
  • Circular Economy

Our Mission

Turning environmental challenges into sustainable opportunities.

We exist to protect the environment through science and precision.

Our mission is to develop advanced recovery and refining technologies that eliminate hazardous waste, reduce emissions, and unlock new economic value from industrial by-products, enabling a truly circular economy.

Each project we undertake reflects our core values: integrity, accountability, and quiet excellence. Our work speaks through data, not promotion.

Our Philosophy

Where science meets responsibility

OneCycle was founded to align technological innovation with environmental stewardship. Every process we design is based on evidence, precision, and long- term responsibility, ensuring that sustainability is not a slogan but a measurable outcome.

We operate discreetly, collaborating with selected partners who share our values of integrity, transparency, and purpose-driven innovation. Our goal is not visibility, but verified results.

Our Experience

25+ years of combined expertise in sustainable materials recovery

Our team brings together experience in materials science, chemistry, and environmental engineering. Across decades of combined research and industrial application, we've refined proprietary systems capable of achieving over 90% reduction in hazardous waste and near-zero in-house waste generation.

Through collaboration with partners, institutions, and industry leaders, OneCycle continues to push the boundaries of sustainable refining and environmental remediation.
