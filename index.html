A cleaner material cycle

Waste is not the end of a material’s story. With the right science, it becomes the beginning of its next use.

OneCycle combines precision engineering with environmental stewardship to convert complex industrial residues into refined, reusable materials.

Every solution is tailored, data-led, and designed around long-term commercial and environmental value.

Our capabilities

From industrial residue to reusable material.

OneCycle develops and validates processes for materials that are difficult to recover, refine, or make safe. Each project begins with the material and its environmental context.

01

Advanced material recovery

Recovery of high-value elements from hazardous industrial by-products and residues using clean, data-led processes.

02

Sustainable refining

Low-emission refining methods developed around the material, required purity, efficiency, and compliance needs.

03

Environmental remediation

Conversion of hazardous materials into reusable assets, supporting safer land, water, and industrial environments.

04

Clean-technology research and development

Development of electrochemical and material-processing technologies from early research through pilot validation.

Our approach

Science and sustainability, working as one.

A disciplined route from material assessment to industrial application.

We combine materials science, process engineering, and environmental responsibility. The objective is straightforward: establish what can be recovered, validate how it can be done responsibly, and develop a route that can scale.

01

Analyse

02

Engineer

Develop and validate a process around performance, safety, and compliance.

03

Scale

Move from validated method to pilot and industrial application.
