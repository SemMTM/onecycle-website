Sustainable Recovery and Refining Solutions

Turning hazardous materials into valuable resources through precision, science, and responsibility.

OneCycle develops and delivers technologies that convert industrial by-products and hazardous materials into reusable, valuable resources. Our recovery and refining systems are designed to minimise waste, reduce emissions, and create measurable environmental and economic impact, all grounded in data, integrity, and scientific precision.

Our Core Capabilities.

Each service combines clean-technology innovation with practical application, built to achieve real sustainability outcomes.

Advanced Material Recovery

Recovering value from industrial waste.

We extract and repurpose high-value materials from complex residues using clean, science-driven recovery processes. Each system is engineered to maximise environmental impact reduction while maximising material yield and resource circularity.

  • Converts hazardous residues into reusable materials.
  • Significantly reduces landfill dependency.
  • Enables measurable ESG and compliance progress.

Sustainable Refining Solutions

Low-emission refining for a cleaner future.

Our sustainable refining methods are designed to optimise yield and purity while reducing emissions and energy consumption. Each solution is tailored to client-specific materials, ensuring high efficiency and full regulatory compliance.

  • Energy-efficient refining technologies.
  • Minimal waste and emissions.
  • Compliance with global environmental standards.

Environmental Remediation & Circular Projects

Restoring environments. Creating value.

We partner with industries to transform hazardous materials into reusable assets, restoring land and water quality while supporting circular-economy goals. Every project is approached with precision, responsibility, and transparency.

  • Conversion of hazardous materials into safe resources.
  • Long-term site restoration and sustainability outcomes.
  • Circular-economy integration for measurable impact.

Research & Development in Clean-Technology Innovation

Innovation through science.

From concept to pilot scale, our R&D team advances electrochemical and material-processing technologies that redefine sustainable recovery and refining. Collaboration, precision, and scalability are central to every innovation we deliver.

  • Proprietary electrochemical process development.
  • Scalable pilot systems for industrial application.
  • Continuous innovation for circular industry models.

Specialised Support for Complex Challenges.

Beyond core technologies, we provide technical expertise to help partners validate, optimise, and integrate sustainable systems with confidence.

Laboratory Analysis & Process Validation
