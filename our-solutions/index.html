Our solutions

Sustainable recovery and refining solutions

Precision with purpose

Turning hazardous materials into valuable resources through precision, science and responsibility.

Built for measurable impact

OneCycle develops and delivers technologies that convert industrial by-products and hazardous materials into reusable, valuable resources.

Our recovery and refining systems are designed to minimise waste, reduce emissions and create measurable environmental and economic impact — all grounded in data, integrity and scientific precision.

Core capabilities

Four disciplines. One circular system.

01

Advanced material recovery Recovering value from industrial waste.

We extract and repurpose high-value materials from complex residues using clean, science-driven recovery processes. Each system is engineered to maximise environmental impact reduction while maximising material yield and resource circularity.

  • Converts hazardous residues into reusable materials
  • Significantly reduces landfill dependency
  • Enables measurable ESG and compliance progress
02

Sustainable refining solutions Low-emission refining for a cleaner future.

Our refining technologies optimise yield and purity while reducing emissions, energy use and waste. Each solution is tailored to the materials, operational requirements and regulatory environment of the project.

  • Energy-efficient refining technologies
  • Minimal waste and emissions
  • Compliance with global environmental standards
03

Environmental remediation Restoring environments. Creating value.

We transform hazardous materials into reusable assets while supporting the restoration of affected land and water. The result is a practical route from legacy liability to circular value.

  • Conversion of hazardous materials into safe resources
  • Long-term site restoration and sustainability
  • Circular-economy integration
04

Clean-technology R&D Innovation through science.

From concept to pilot scale, we develop electrochemical and material-processing technologies with collaboration, precision and scalability built into every stage.

  • Proprietary electrochemical process development
  • Scalable pilot systems
  • Continuous innovation
