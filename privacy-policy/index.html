Privacy policy

How we handle your data

A clear account of the information we collect and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to enquiries submitted through the OneCycle website.

Questions about your data

1. Introduction

OneCycle Noble Metals Ltd (“OneCycle”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data in a transparent and secure manner.

This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data we collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website contact form, we collect:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Email address
  • Company or organisation, if provided
  • Subject of enquiry
  • Message content

No other personal data is collected through the website.

3. How your data is collected

Your information is collected when you voluntarily submit the contact form. Form submissions are processed using Netlify Forms.

We do not collect personal data through user accounts, cookies for advertising or third-party tracking profiles.

4. How we use your data

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Communicate with you regarding your message
  • Assess potential collaborations, partnerships or technical discussions

We do not use your information for marketing lists, automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Legitimate interest — to respond to enquiries and conduct business communications
  • Consent — when you voluntarily submit information through the contact form

6. Data storage and security

  • Form submissions are securely handled and stored by Netlify
  • Reasonable technical and organisational measures protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse
  • Access to submitted data is restricted to authorised personnel

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Your information is shared only with Netlify as our form-processing provider, or where disclosure is required by law.

8. International data transfers

Netlify may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where this occurs, appropriate safeguards are used in line with applicable data-protection law.

9. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to respond to an enquiry and maintain appropriate business records. Data is then securely deleted when it is no longer required.

10. Your rights

Under UK data-protection law, you may:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

11. Contact information

OneCycle Noble Metals Ltd

Email: info@onecyclegroup.com

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
