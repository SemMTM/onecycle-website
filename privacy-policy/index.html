Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

OneCycle Noble Metals Ltd (“OneCycle”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data submitted through our website.

We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website contact form, we collect the following information:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Email address
  • Company / organisation (if provided)
  • Subject of enquiry
  • Message content

No other personal data is collected through the website.

3. How Your Data Is Collected

All form submissions on this website are processed using Netlify Forms, a service provided by Netlify, Inc.

Data is collected only when you voluntarily submit information via a contact form.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely for the purpose of:

  • Responding to your enquiry
  • Communicating with you regarding your message
  • Assessing potential collaborations, partnerships, or technical discussions

We do not use your data for marketing purposes, mailing lists, or automated profiling.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Legitimate interest – to respond to enquiries and conduct business communications
  • Consent – when you voluntarily submit information via the contact form

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Form submissions are securely handled and stored by Netlify
  • Reasonable technical and organisational measures are in place to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse
  • Access to submitted data is restricted to authorised personnel only

We do not knowingly store personal data longer than necessary.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

Your information is shared only with:

  • Netlify, as the form processing provider

No other third parties receive your data unless legally required.

8. International Data Transfers

Netlify may process data outside the United Kingdom.

Where this occurs, appropriate safeguards are in place to ensure compliance with UK GDPR.

9. Data Retention

We retain enquiry data only for as long as necessary to:

  • Respond to the enquiry
  • Maintain appropriate business records

Data that is no longer required is securely deleted.

10. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

11. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact:

OneCycle Noble Metals Ltd
Email: info@onecyclegroup.com

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or business practices.

The most recent version will always be available on this website.
